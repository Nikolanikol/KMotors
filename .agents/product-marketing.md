

# Product Marketing Context — KMotors / K-Axis

## What we sell

Two product lines on kmotors.shop (brand: K-Axis, rebrand in progress):

1. **Used Korean cars** — sourced directly from Encar (Korea's largest car marketplace), exported worldwide
2. **Genuine OEM parts** — Hyundai / Kia / Genesis (Mobis), 140,000+ positions, shipped from Korea via EMS

## Who we are

Licensed car dealer and registered sole proprietorship based in Asan, South Korea. We operate from inside Korea — we buy at local prices from local sources, not through middlemen. Founder-led: the person you message is the person who sources your car or part.

## Target audience

- **Cars**: buyers in CIS markets (Russia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Georgia, Armenia) and UAE importing Korean used cars; both end buyers and resellers/importers
- **Parts**: owners of Hyundai/Kia/Genesis vehicles who can't find genuine parts locally or are overpaying for them; also small workshops and parts resellers

## Positioning

Direct-from-Korea exporter with real local presence. Not a dropshipper, not an aggregator — a licensed dealer physically in Korea with direct access to Encar auctions and the OEM parts supply chain.

## Key advantages (in order)

1. **Price** — buying at Korean domestic prices, transparent markup; import duty calculator on site so buyers see full landed cost upfront
2. **Service, always reachable** — direct line to the founder via Telegram/WhatsApp; no ticket systems, no call centers; answers in your language
3. **Flexibility** — custom sourcing on request: specific car from Encar by link, rare OEM part by VIN or part number; individual shipping arrangements (EMS parcel optimization to minimize cost)
4. **Deals** — favorable offers on popular positions; parts bundles ship together to cut per-item delivery cost
5. **Loyalty** — returning customers get better terms; long-term relationships over one-off sales

## Proof points

- Licensed dealer, registered business in South Korea (사업자등록)
- Direct Encar sourcing with price analysis tools
- 140,000+ genuine OEM positions in catalog
- All parts photos show actual items (own de-branded photo pipeline)
- Import duty calculator for Russia/Uzbekistan built into the site

## Tone of voice

Direct, practical, no fluff. We talk like a knowledgeable friend in the car business, not a corporation. Russian content can be informal; English stays professional but human. Never overpromise delivery times or availability.

## What we DON'T do

- No salvage/flood-damaged cars sold as clean
- No aftermarket parts sold as OEM
- No hidden fees — landed cost is calculated openly
